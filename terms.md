# Rings Wallet terms of use
Last updated: 24 November 2026

These terms cover your use of Rings Wallet. They are written to be read: short clauses, plain words.

### About these terms

1. These terms govern your use of Rings Wallet, a self-custodial Solana wallet made by Rings (rings.run), as a browser

2. By installing or using Rings Wallet, you agree to these terms. If you don't agree, don't use it.
3. How the wallet handles your data is set out in the Rings Wallet privacy policy (rings.run/wallet/privacy).
4. Rings Wallet's private balance runs on Solana Privacy Rings, built and operated by Helius under its own terms. Rings
   is independent and not affiliated with Helius.

### Who can use Rings Wallet

1. You must be of legal age where you live and able to enter a binding agreement.
2. You may not use Rings Wallet from a place, or on behalf of a person, that sanctions or local law prohibit.
3. Rings screens wallets against the US sanctions list and may refuse its services, such as swaps, to a listed wallet.

### Your keys

1. Rings Wallet is self-custodial. Your recovery phrase and keys are made and kept on your device, encrypted with your
   password. Rings never has them.
2. Rings can't recover your recovery phrase, your keys or your password. If you lose them, Rings can't restore your
   wallet or your funds.
3. Keep your recovery phrase offline and private. Anyone who has it can take your funds.
4. You're responsible for your device, your password and every transaction signed with your keys.

### Transactions

1. You approve every transaction. The review screen shows what moves, the fees and who is asking before you confirm.
2. Transactions on Solana are final. Rings can't stop, change or reverse one after you sign it.
3. Sites and apps you connect to are not run by Rings, unless they are Rings sites, and have their own terms. On a phone,
   an app states its own name, which the wallet can't confirm. Only sign what you understand.
4. Prices, balances and previews come from third parties and can be wrong or late. They are information, not advice.
   Rings Wallet doesn't give investment, legal or tax advice.
5. You're responsible for taxes and reporting that apply to your transactions.

### Private balance and swaps

1. Private transfers hide their amounts, but moving coins into or out of your private balance is public, as the privacy
   policy explains.
2. Swaps are made by Rings' relay through Jupiter. A swap fills within the slippage you allow, or is refunded to your
   balance.

### Fees

1. Rings Wallet charges 0.1% on every send, every swap and every move between your Public and Private balances. It is
   paid on top of the amount, in the same coin, inside the same transaction, and the review screen shows it before you
   confirm.
2. A swap's fee is not returned if the swap is refunded.
3. Network fees are paid to the network, not to Rings. Transactions that sites and apps ask the wallet to sign carry no
   Rings Wallet fee.

2. Move funds you know or suspect come from crime, or help someone else do so.
3. Interfere with Rings, its servers or its relay, or try to get around its fees or limits.
